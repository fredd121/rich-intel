About Us

Rich Intel — a school business project

Company Description

Rich Intel is a student business team focused on developing practical, affordable, and eco-friendly household products. Our featured product is CalGuava Drain Flow, created as a natural alternative for regular drain maintenance.

How It Started

The idea started from the common problem of unpleasant-smelling and lightly clogged household drains. We developed a powdered product using calamansi and guava leaves together with cleaning ingredients to create a convenient drain-maintenance solution.

Our Product

CalGuava Drain Flow

Product Description

CalGuava Drain Flow is a powdered drain-cleaning and maintenance product made with calamansi and guava leaves. It is designed to help reduce foul odors, light grease, and minor buildup in kitchen, bathroom, and floor drains. Its easy-to-use powdered formula is convenient for regular drain maintenance.
